01 / Overview

A single canvas for
every creative signal.

Existing video systems often split generation, extension, reference control, and editing across separate models. Vorch-Omni reframes them as one conditional denoising problem.

Video and audio can each be a clean condition or a noisy target. Token-level masks, task identifiers, and position types tell the model what to generate, preserve, or reference—and whether an input belongs to the target timeline or stands independently.
